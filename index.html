Celestial Navigation

Learn how to find north, south, east and west using just the stars. No previous knowledge needed.

💡 Why this is here

Before GPS, people used the stars to navigate across the desert or the Gulf. These skills can still be used today if your phone has no signal or battery. They will also help you understand how the sky works: why stars move, why one star does not move, and why the sky looks different depending on your location.

👆 How to use it
  1. Select a mission tab. Start with Mission 1, as the missions build on each other.

  3. Press Next step. This will add one item to the picture on the left. Follow along.

03

Celestial Coordinates

Find out how astronomers locate stars accurately.

💡 Why this is here

To locate a star, you need to know where to look. The terms on these cards are used in instructions like “look 30 degrees up in the south-east”, and all star apps use them too. Altitude and azimuth tell you where to look from your location. Right ascension and declination give a fixed location for each star, and are used in star charts and catalogues.

👆 How to use it
  1. Tap a card to flip it over and read the meaning of the term. Tap it again to flip it back.
  2. Drag the star in the dome on the right.
  3. Watch the numbers change. Altitude tells you how high the star is (0 degrees = horizon, 90 degrees = directly overhead). Azimuth tells you which compass direction you are facing (0 degrees = north, 90 degrees = east, 180 degrees = south, 270 degrees = west).
  4. Challenge: move the star to 45 degrees altitude in the south-east (135 degrees).

04

Light Pollution & Conditions

See how the stars are hidden by city lights, and find out what else affects how much you can see.

💡 Why this is here

Light from roads and buildings makes the sky glow, so faint stars are difficult to see. Most people in the UAE live in bright coastal cities, so the best thing you can do to see more stars is to go somewhere dark. Astronomers use the Bortle scale to measure how dark the sky is, from 1 (darkest desert) to 9 (city centre). The weather and the Moon also affect how much you can see.

👆 How to use it
  1. Slide the bar to the left, from the city (9) to the desert (1).
  2. Watch the picture: more stars appear, and once the sky is dark enough you will see the Milky Way.
  3. Read the text underneath the bar. It tells you what the sky looks like and where in the UAE you would find this.
  4. Tap the 5 buttons below to find out what else affects what you can see.

05

The UAE Sky Calendar

Discover what you can see each month and when the weather will be best.

💡 Why this is here

The sky changes throughout the year as Earth orbits the Sun, so we face a different part of space each month. The UAE’s weather also changes a lot. The best time to stargaze is during the cool, clear winters, while the hottest and most humid months can be quite challenging. Knowing this helps you pick the best night to stargaze.

👆 How to use it
  1. Click on the month you would like to view. The current month is highlighted when the page opens.
  2. Discover which constellations are visible in the evening, whether there are any special events, and what the typical weather is.
  3. The stars in the centre of the wheel show how good the month is for stargazing: ★★★★★ = best.
  4. Click Open this sky in the Sky Explorer to open the star map for that month.

06

Tools & Technology

Free and paid apps and websites to help you locate stars and plan your night.

💡 Why this is here

Technology plays a large role in modern astronomy. Apps use your phone’s GPS and motion sensors to identify the stars you are pointing at. Websites can predict clear nights and locate dark areas. This means beginners can do in minutes what used to take years to learn. This is a great example of the scientific and technical innovation behind this project.

👆 How to use it
  1. Read through each card to see what each tool does and what it is best used for.
  2. Start by using one planning tool (Stellarium) and one weather tool (Clear Outside).
  3. If you are using an app outside, switch it to red mode or night mode if available, and reduce your screen brightness. Bright screens stop your eyes adapting to the dark.
07

About This Project

🎯 Learning goal

To increase my knowledge of celestial navigation and constellation mapping throughout the UAE.

🌍 Global context

Scientific and technical innovation. Digital tools are used to make scientific knowledge easy for everyone to access, so anyone can navigate the night sky.

✨ Why the UAE?

The visibility of stars varies greatly across the UAE because of artificial lighting, buildings, location, humidity and dust. Comparing different locations shows how the environment affects what you can see.
